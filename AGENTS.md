# Exhibition development

This is a public-facing fan exhibition with a curated collection. Keep private
questions, agent answers, raw prompts, transcripts, listening timestamps, and local
source paths out of this repository. Do not research the novel online. New factual
captions must arrive through the companion's local evidence and export workflow.
The creator is still reading; a finished-book audience never authorizes reading
ahead or inventing later exhibition contents.

Preserve the explicit spoiler-warning foyer. Do not load collection images before
acceptance. Assume visitors have finished the book; do not add listening controls.
Keep in-book attribution, author attribution, and digital realization separate.
Personal notes are curated reflections, not a conversation archive. Do not invent
first-person statements for the curator.

Use a high-contrast dark theme, with locally brighter or darker spaces where
supported descriptions call for them. The prototype's room arrangement is an
interpretation. Preserve image aspect ratios and do not double-frame images.
Keep the accessible Collection view complete when changing the 3D experience.
Respect reduced motion, readable text, touch navigation, and keyboard focus.

Run `npm run build` and `npm test` for behavior changes. Inspect the browser on
desktop and mobile for visual changes. Do not claim an unrun check passed. Do not
publish, push, or change repository visibility without user authorization for it.
